## Szöveg (World English Bible)

Forrás: Bible Hub / thebible.ch / BibleStudyTools WEB-oldalak, 2026-11-06-i ellenőrzés. Az 1. és 5. vers mindhárom helyen azonos.

- **9:1** `As he passed by, he saw a man blind from birth.`
- **9:2** `His disciples asked him, "Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?"`
- **9:3** `Jesus answered, "This man didn't sin, nor did his parents, but that the works of God might be revealed in him.`
- **9:4** `I must work the works of him who sent me while it is day. The night is coming, when no one can work.`
- **9:5** `While I am in the world, I am the light of the world."`

Fordítási eltérés: a 3. versben a WEB régebbi kiadása szerint `Neither did this man sin, nor his parents; but, that the works of God might be revealed in him.` Itt a modernebb szövegváltozatot használjuk. Ha a 6–7. verset is bevonjuk, előbb ugyanazt a kiadást kell ellenőrizni, mert a 6–7. vers szövegét csak egy régebbi kiadást mutató tükörről olvastam, a mai ellenőrzésnél nem volt két forrás.

Csak hallgatás, tanári kérdés-válasz (bejelentve), nincs elvárt válasz. Tanári összefoglaló, külön jelölve:

Nehéz szavak (`sinned`, `works of God`, `revealed`) ma **nem** tananyag. Későbbi szombatra marad.

## Szómagyarázatok (mindegyik négy sebességgel)

### blind
`Blind means a person cannot see. The eyes do not work. A blind man cannot see the sun. A blind woman cannot read a book with her eyes. Another way to say it: a person who cannot see. Blind does not mean deaf. Deaf means a person cannot hear. I ask, I answer. Can a blind man see? No. Can a deaf man hear? No. Is a blind person a person who cannot see or cannot hear? Cannot see. We will hear this word in the Bible text.`

### passed by (As he passed by)
`Pass by means: you go near a person or a place, and you do not stop. Tom passes by the help desk on his way to the office. The bus passes by my house. Another way to say it: he walked near. Passed is in the past. Today I pass by. Yesterday I passed by. Pass by does not mean stay. I ask, I answer. If you pass by a shop, do you go in? No, you go near it. Is "passed by" now or in the past? In the past.`

### the light of the world
`Light is what helps you see. The sun gives light. A lamp gives light. The world is the earth and all the people on it. The light of the world means: the one who gives light to all people. Here, light is a picture. It means help, truth, and a way to see. Jesus says: I am the light of the world. Remember Genesis: God said, "Let there be light." I ask, I answer. Does a lamp give light? Yes. Is "the light of the world" a small lamp or a picture for Jesus? A picture for Jesus. (Teacher: this is my explanation, not the Bible text.)`

## Támogató szavak (rövid mondatokban, nem külön blokkban)

- `from birth`: `He was blind from birth. He was blind when he was born.`
- `while`: `While it is day, we can work. While means at the same time.`

## Szkript (példa-fordulatok)

1. `T: Genesis. Repeat: In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.` (építkezéssel, ha elakad)
2. `T: Listen to a new verse. As he passed by, he saw a man blind from birth.` (négy kör)
3. `T: Say only: As he passed by.` → `L: As he passed by.` → `T: Add: he saw a man.` → `T: Add: blind from birth.` → `T: Say the whole sentence.`
4. `T: Listen. While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.` (négy kör)
5. `T: Say only: While I am in the world.` → `T: Add: I am the light of the world.` → `T: Say the whole verse.`
6. `T: Answer: Who saw a man? Answer with one word.` · `T: Answer: yes or no. Was the man blind?` · `T: Answer with a sentence. What does Jesus say about himself?`
7. Hibák: `light` / `right`, hiányzó `the`, `blind` ejtése. Ha a mondat két kísérlet után sem megy, bontsd kisebb részre.
