# LESSONS_W01_IT.md — 1. hét, IT-sáv, L1–L5 teljes szkript

Kiindulás: IT A1+ / korai A2. A módszer lépései: `SESSION_SCRIPTS.md`. Minden magyarázat és történet itt készen van, a tutor szó szerint használhatja. A tanulónak szóló szöveg **angol**; a magyar megjegyzés a tutornak szól.

# L1 — The locked account (vasárnap, 45')

**Cél:** 3 szókapcsolat megértése és kimondása.
**Új szókapcsolatok:** `My account is locked.` · `I forgot my password.` · `Please stop trying.` (ismétlés a szeptemberi anyagból)
**Támogató szavak:** account, locked, forgot, help desk. **Ismétlő mini-blokk:** reset the password, stop trying.
**Idő:** Kérdéstorna 5' · szavak 14' · történet 7' · bővítés 6' · aktív feladatok 10' · összefoglaló 3'.

## A. Szómagyarázatok (mindegyik 4 sebességgel)

### account
`An account is your name and your password for a system. With your account, you can log in. Tom has an email account. Anna has a work account. Another way to say it: your place in a system. An account is not a person. An account is not a computer. I ask, I answer. Is an account a person? No. Do you need an account to log in? Yes. Is a password part of an account or part of a printer? Part of an account. We will hear this word in the story.`

### locked
`A door can be locked. When a door is locked, you cannot open it. An account can be locked, too. When an account is locked, you cannot use it. Tom's account is locked. He cannot log in. Locked does not mean deleted. The account is still there. You just cannot use it now. I ask, I answer. Can you open a locked door? No. Is a locked account deleted? No. Is a locked account open or closed? Closed. We will hear this in the story.`

### I forgot my password. (forgot)
`Forgot means: you do not remember. Tom does not remember his password. He forgot it. Anna forgot her key. She does not remember where the key is. Another way to say it: I do not remember. Forgot does not mean lost. The password is not lost. It is in your head, but you cannot find it. I ask, I answer. Does Tom remember his password? No. He forgot it. Did Anna forget her key or remember it? She forgot it. We will hear this in the story.`

### help desk
`A help desk is a place, or a phone number, where you ask for help with computers. Tom calls the help desk. Anna works at the help desk. Another way to say it: the IT support. A help desk is not a restaurant. A help desk is not a shop. I ask, I answer. Does Anna help people with computers? Yes. Is a help desk in a shop or is it a computer support? A computer support. We will hear this in the story.`

### Ismétlő blokk (egy lassú és egy természetes menet, rövid)
- `Try means: you do something to see if it works. Tom tries the old password. It does not work. Stop trying means: do not try again. Stop. I ask, I answer. Does stop trying mean try again? No.`
- `Reset the password means: make a new password. Anna resets the password. Now Tom has a new password. I ask, I answer. Does Anna delete the password or make a new password? She makes a new password.`

## B. Mini-történet (négyszer: lassú, lassú, közepes, természetes)

```
Tom works in sales.
This morning, Tom cannot log in.
He sees a message: "Your account is locked."
Tom forgot his password.
He tries the old password three times.
Now the account is locked.
Tom calls the help desk.
Anna works in IT.
Anna says, "Please stop trying."
Anna resets the password.
Now Tom can log in.
```

## C. Bővítés (bemenet)

- **Átfogalmazás:** `Tom cannot use his account. The account does not work. Tom does not remember his password. He tries the old password three times. Anna helps Tom. She makes a new password for him.`
- **Tagadás és ellentét:** `Tom does not work in IT. Tom works in sales. Anna does not work in sales. Anna works in IT. The problem is not with a printer. The problem is with the account. The account is not deleted. It is locked.`
- **Tanár kérdez és felel (bejelentve):** `I ask, I answer. Is Tom's account open? No. It is locked. Who works in IT? Anna works in IT. What does Tom forget? His password. Who says, "Please stop trying"? Anna says it.`
- **Másik nézőpont:** `I'm Tom. I work in sales. This morning I cannot log in. I forgot my password. I try the old password three times. My account is locked. I call the help desk.`

## D. Aktív feladatok (létra, a válasz soha nincs megadva)

- Igen/nem: Is Tom in sales? · Is the account locked? · Does Anna work in sales? · Does Tom remember his password?
- A vagy B: Does Tom work in sales or in IT? · Is the problem with the account or with the printer? · Does Anna say "Please stop trying" or "Please try again"?
- Egy szó: Who works in IT? · Who forgot the password? · What is locked?
- Mondat: What is the problem? · What does Anna say? · What does Tom forget?

## E. Építkezés (mindhárom kulcsmondat)

1. `locked` → `is locked` → `account is locked` → `My account is locked.`
2. `password` → `my password` → `forgot my password` → `I forgot my password.`
3. `trying` → `stop trying` → `Please stop trying.`

Gyakori hibák: hiányzó `is` (`my account locked`), `I forget` helyett `I forgot`, `please stop try` helyett `trying`.

## F. Irányított variáció (egy elemet cserélj)

- `My account is locked.` → `My phone is locked.` → `The door is locked.` → `My email account is locked.`
- `I forgot my password.` → `I forgot my user name.` → `I forgot my key.` → `I forgot my phone.`
- `Please stop trying.` → `Please stop.` → `Please stop calling.`

## G. Támogatott szerepjáték

`T: You are Tom. You call the help desk. I am Anna. I say: Help desk. Say one: My account is locked. / I forgot my password.` Majd: `Anna: Please stop trying.` és a tanuló válaszol: `OK.`/`Thank you.` Második kör: csak a két mondat, felsorolás nélkül.

## H. Összefoglaló és rögzítés

Három mondat együtt: `My account is locked. I forgot my password. Please stop trying.`
SESSION RECORD: mely tételek mentek elsőre, melyek függőben, hibák (`is`, `forgot`), visszahozás: +1, +3, +7 nap.

# L2 — Questions at the help desk (hétfő, 45')

**Cél:** a `Who / What` kérdések megértése és a három kérdőmondat használata.
**Új szókapcsolatok:** `Who is calling?` · `What is the problem?` · `What is your user name?`
**Támogató szavak:** user name, problem, answer the phone.
**Visszahozás:** L1 három mondata (kérdéstorna elején).

## A. Szómagyarázatok

### user name
`A user name is the name you type to log in. Tom's user name is "tom". Anna's user name is "anna". Another way to say it: your login name. A user name is not a password. The password is secret. The user name is not secret. I ask, I answer. Do you type your user name to log in? Yes. Is a user name secret or not secret? Not secret.`

### problem
`A problem is something that does not work. Tom's account is locked. That is a problem. A broken printer is a problem. A cold coffee is not an IT problem. Another way to say it: something is wrong. I ask, I answer. Is a locked account a problem? Yes. Is a good day a problem? No.`

### Who is calling?
`On the phone, we say: Who is calling? We want to know the name of the person who calls. Tom calls. Anna asks: Who is calling? Tom says: This is Tom. Who asks about a person. I ask, I answer. Who asks about a person or a thing? A person. Is "Who is calling?" about a person or a place? A person.`

### What is the problem? / What is your user name?
`What asks about a thing. What is the problem? The account is locked. What is your user name? My user name is tom. What does not ask about a person. I ask, I answer. Does What ask about a thing or about a person? A thing.`

## B. Mini-történet (négyszer)

```
Tom calls the help desk.
Anna answers the phone.
Anna asks, "Who is calling?"
Tom says, "This is Tom."
Anna asks, "What is the problem?"
Tom says, "My account is locked. I forgot my password."
Anna asks, "What is your user name?"
Tom says, "My user name is tom."
Anna says, "Please stop trying."
```

## C. Bővítés

- **Átfogalmazás:** `Tom calls the help desk. Anna answers the phone. She wants to know his name. She wants to know the problem. She wants to know his user name.`
- **Tagadás:** `Anna does not ask about the printer. She asks about the account. Tom does not say "I am Anna." He says "This is Tom."`
- **Tanár kérdez és felel:** `I ask, I answer. Who asks the questions? Anna asks the questions. Who answers the questions? Tom answers. What is Tom's problem? His account is locked. What is his user name? His user name is tom.`
- **Másik nézőpont (Anna):** `I'm Anna. I work at the help desk. A man calls. I ask, "Who is calling?" He says, "This is Tom." I ask, "What is the problem?"`

## D. Aktív feladatok

- Igen/nem: Does Anna ask about a printer? · Is Tom the user? · Is Anna the user?
- A vagy B: Does Anna ask "Who is calling?" or "Where is Tom?" · Is "tom" a user name or a password?
- Egy szó: Who asks the questions? · Who calls the help desk? · What does Tom say his user name is?
- Mondat: What is the problem? · What does Anna ask first? · What is Tom's user name?

**Kérdőszó-hallási mini-feladat (2'):** a tanár: `Who? What? What? Who?` Válasz: `person` vagy `thing`.

## E. Építkezés

1. `calling` → `is calling` → `Who is calling?`
2. `problem` → `the problem` → `What is the problem?`
3. `user name` → `your user name` → `What is your user name?`

## F. Irányított variáció

- `What is the problem?` → `What is the name?` → `What is the password?`
- `Who is calling?` → `Who is speaking?` → `Who is there?`
- Válaszok: `This is Tom.` → `This is Maria.` → `This is [saját név].`

## G. Szerepjáték

1. A tanuló **Tom**, a tutor **Anna** (három kérdés, a tanuló válaszol, a válaszok megadva listából: `My account is locked. / I forgot my password. / My user name is tom.`).
2. Szerepcsere: a tanuló **Anna**, a tutor **Tom**; a tanuló feltesz három kérdést: `Who is calling? What is the problem? What is your user name?`

## H. Összefoglaló és rögzítés

# L3 — Yesterday: the same story in the past (kedd, 45')

**Cél:** a történet múlt idejű változata, kulcsmondatok.
**Új szókapcsolatok:** `I tried the old password.` · `Now you can log in.` · `It works now.`
**Támogató szavak:** yesterday, logged in, worked.
**Visszahozás:** `I reset the password.` (félig aktív), L1–L2 kérdései.

## A. Szómagyarázatok

### Yesterday / past (idő)
`Today is today. Yesterday is the day before today. Yesterday was Monday. Today is Tuesday. When something happened yesterday, it is in the past. In the past, many words change. Today I try. Yesterday I tried. Today I call. Yesterday I called. Today I say. Yesterday I said. I ask, I answer. Is yesterday before today or after today? Before today. Is "I tried" now or in the past? In the past.`

### I tried the old password.
`Tried is the past of try. Today, Tom tries a password. Yesterday, Tom tried a password. The old password is the password from before. The new password is the password after the reset. I ask, I answer. Is the old password new or from before? From before. Did Tom try the password yesterday or today? Yesterday.`

### Now you can log in. / It works now.
`Log in means: you enter your user name and password, and you can use the system. Now you can log in means: it is possible. It works means: it is OK, there is no problem. It works now means: the problem is gone. Before, it did not work. Now it works. I ask, I answer. Does it work now? Yes. Was there a problem before? Yes.`

### Ismétlő blokk: reset
`Anna reset the password. Reset is the same today and yesterday: today I reset, yesterday I reset. I ask, I answer. Did Anna delete the password or make a new one? A new one.`

## B. Mini-történet (négyszer)

```
Yesterday morning, Tom forgot his password.
He tried the old password three times.
The account was locked.
Tom called the help desk.
Anna answered the phone.
Anna said, "Please stop trying."
Anna reset the password.
She said, "Now you can log in."
Tom logged in.
It worked.
```

## C. Bővítés

- **Kontraszt (jelen és múlt egymás mellett):** `Today, Tom tries the password. Yesterday, Tom tried the password. Today, Anna says stop. Yesterday, Anna said stop. Today, Tom calls. Yesterday, Tom called.`
- **Tagadás:** `Tom did not call on Monday. He called yesterday morning. Anna did not delete the account. She reset the password.`
- **Tanár kérdez és felel:** `I ask, I answer. Did Tom forget his password? Yes. Did Anna reset it? Yes. Who called the help desk? Tom. What did Anna say? Please stop trying. Does it work now? Yes.`
- **Másik nézőpont:** `I'm Tom. Yesterday I forgot my password. I tried the old password three times. My account was locked. I called the help desk.`

## D. Aktív feladatok

- Igen/nem: Did Tom forget his password? · Did Anna delete the account? · Did Tom log in at the end?
- A vagy B: Did Tom try the old password or the new password? · Did Anna say "Please stop trying" or "Please try again"?
- Egy szó: Who reset the password? · Who called the help desk? · What did Tom try?
- Mondat: What did Tom forget? · What did Anna say? · What happened at the end?

## E. Építkezés

1. `old password` → `the old password` → `tried the old password` → `I tried the old password.`
2. `log in` → `can log in` → `Now you can log in.`
3. `works` → `works now` → `It works now.`

## F. Múlt idő irányított variáció (szabály nélkül, minta alapján)

`Today I try. Yesterday I tried.` → `Today I call. Yesterday I called.` → `Today I say. Yesterday I said.` → `Today I forget. Yesterday I forgot.` → `Today I reset. Yesterday I reset.`
Cél: a tanuló maga mondja: `Yesterday I [past verb] ...`

## G. Szerepjáték

`T: Yesterday you called the help desk. I am Anna. Say one: I forgot my password. / I tried the old password.` Majd: `Anna: Now you can log in.` `L: It works now.`

## H. Összefoglaló és rögzítés (múlt idejű hibák: `tried`, `said`, `called`)

# L4 — Anna's view, articles, new checks (szerda, 30')

**Cél:** nézőpontváltás, `a / an / the`, két új mondat.
**Új szókapcsolatok:** `Where are you?` · `I'll check your account.`
**Rövid lecke:** nincs új történet-sorozat, mert a hangsúly a visszahozáson van.

## A. Szómagyarázatok

### Where are you?
`Where asks about a place. Tom is at his desk. Anna asks: Where are you? Tom says: I am at my desk. Where does not ask about a person. Where does not ask about a thing. I ask, I answer. Does Where ask about a place? Yes. Is "at my desk" a place? Yes.`

### I'll check your account.
`I'll is a short form of I will. I'll check means: I will look and see. Anna says: I'll check your account. She looks at the account. She sees: it is locked. Check does not mean change. Check means look. I ask, I answer. Does Anna change the account when she checks? No. She looks.`

### a / an / the (rövid blokk)
`Use a or an for one thing. Tom has an account. Anna has a desk. Use an before a vowel sound: an account, an error, an IT manager. Use the when we know which one. The account is locked. The password is new. I ask, I answer. "Tom has ___ account": a or an? An. "___ account is locked": a or the? The.`

## B. Mini-történet Anna nézőpontjából (négyszer)

```
I'm Anna. I work in IT.
This morning, a user called me.
His name is Tom. He is at his desk.
I asked, "Where are you?"
He said, "I'm at my desk."
I said, "I'll check your account."
I checked the account. It was locked.
I reset the password.
Now Tom can log in.
```

## C. Bővítés

`Anna looked at the account. It was locked. Anna did not change the account first. She checked it first. Tom is at his desk. He is not at home.` Tanár kérdez és felel: `I ask, I answer. Where is Tom? At his desk. Who checked the account? Anna. Was it locked? Yes.`

## D. Aktív feladatok

Kérdéstorna 2× hosszabb (8') a gyenge Who/Where kérdésekre, majd:
- Egy szó: Where is Tom? · Who checked the account? · What did Anna reset?
- Mondat: Where is Tom? (`He is at his desk.`) · What did Anna say? (`I'll check your account.`)

## E. Névelő-feladat (szóban, 3')

Egy mondat egy feladat: `Say: I'm an IT manager.` · `Say: It's a new password.` · `Say: The account is unlocked.` · `Say: Tom has an account.` · `Say: Anna works in an office.`
Hiba: `I'm IT manager` → `I'm an IT manager.`

## F. Építkezés

1. `where` → `Where are you?`
2. `check` → `I'll check` → `I'll check your account.`

## G. Napló (5')

`Write two or three sentences: What happened in the story?` Javítás: egy névelő, egy múlt idő.
