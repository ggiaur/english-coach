**Szöveg (WEB, ellenőrizve 2026-11-06 a Bible Hub WEB-oldalain):**

```
In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.
God said, "Let there be light," and there was light.
God saw the light, and saw that it was good.
God called the light "day," and the darkness he called "night."
```

2. Építkezés a **2.** mondatra: `Let there be light.` → `God said, "Let there be light."` → `...and there was light.`
3. Építkezés a **4.** mondatra: `God called the light "day."` → `...and the darkness he called "night."`
4. Feladat: `Say the verse where God makes light.` (várt: a 2. mondat)
5. Opcionális (ha a pénteki/szombati zenehallgatás a te döntésed szerint belefér): *Turn! Turn! Turn!* (The Byrds), a szövege a Prédikátor könyve 3. részén alapul; csak a jelentést beszéljük meg.

# SZOMBAT, 2026-11-14 — B1 (60–75')

A kapu dönt: ha a Genesis-mondatok még nem `ACTIVE`-ak (két napon elsőre jók), a lecke főleg ismétlés. A János 9 folytatása csak akkor, ha az 1–5. vers (`LESSON_BIBLE_JOHN9_01.md`) kész.

## 1. Visszahozás (8')

A négy mondat építkezéssel, kérdéstornával: `QUESTION_GYM.md` Biblia-bank.

## 2. Szómagyarázatok (3 új, négy sebességgel)

### created
`Created means made something new. A builder creates a house. An artist creates a picture. Before, there was no house. Then there is a house. Another way to say it: made something new. Created does not mean found. When you find a thing, it was already there. I ask, I answer. Does a builder create a house? Yes. Does a child create a picture or find a picture? Create.`

### called (give a name)
`Call can mean give a name. A mother calls her baby Anna. Anna is the name. God called the light "day." Day is the name of the light. Call does not mean phone here. Call does not mean shout. I ask, I answer. If a mother calls the baby Anna, is Anna the name? Yes. Is the name of the light "day" or "night"? Day.`

### saw
`Saw is the past of see. Today, I see the light. Yesterday, I saw the light. God saw the light. He looked, and he understood: it was good. I ask, I answer. Is saw now or in the past? In the past. Did God see the light? Yes.`

## 3. A szöveg négyszer (lassú, lassú, közepes, természetes)

A négy mondat a péntek szerint. Közben nincs kérdés.

## 4. Bővítés (bemenet, tanári szavak jelölve)

- `Teacher: At first, it was dark. Then God made the light. The light came.`
- `Teacher: God did not call the light "night." God called the light "day."`
- `Teacher: I ask, I answer. Who created the heavens and the earth? God. Who said, "Let there be light"? God. What did God see? The light. What did God call the light? Day.`
- `Teacher: In the Bible, the writer says: God made the light first.`

## 5. Építkezés és aktív feladatok (20')

Építkezés (mondatonként 3–4 lépés), majd kérdések (igen/nem → A vagy B → egy szó → mondat). A választ soha nem adod meg a kérdéssel.

1. Igen/nem: Was it dark at first? · Did God create the light? · Is the dark the same as light?
2. A vagy B: Did God call the light day or night? · Did God call the dark day or night?
3. Egy szó: Who said, "Let there be light"? · What did God see?
4. Mondat: `What did God call the light?` → `God called the light "day."`

## 6. Névelő és múlt idő (5')

`Repeat: God saw the light.` · `Repeat: God created the heavens.` · `Repeat: a bad king.` · `Repeat: He tried to save him.`

## 7. János 9:2–4 megértése (15', ha a gate engedi)

Szöveg (WEB, két forrás egyezik a lényegben; a 3. vers modernebb kiadása):

```
His disciples asked him, "Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?"
Jesus answered, "This man didn't sin, nor did his parents, but that the works of God might be revealed in him.
I must work the works of him who sent me while it is day. The night is coming, when no one can work."
```

Csak hallgatás és megértés. Tanári összefoglaló (jelölve):
`Teacher: The disciples ask a question. They want to know why the man is blind. Jesus answers. He says: the man's blindness is not a punishment. Jesus talks about day and night. Remember: God called the light "day" and the darkness "night."`
Nehéz szavak (`sinned`, `works of God`, `revealed`) ma **nem** tananyag.

## S1 (vasárnap) — What a Wonderful World — Louis Armstrong

**Téma (saját szavakkal):** egy ember a körülötte lévő egyszerű szép dolgokat nézi, nyugodtan és hálásan. Lassú, meleg hang.
**1. kérdés (szöveg nélkül):** `Is the song happy or sad?` (nincs rossz válasz; nyugodt és boldog is jó)
**Szókapcsolatok:**
1. `What a wonderful world!` — `Wonderful means very good. The song says the world is wonderful. We say "What a wonderful world!" when we are happy about the world. We can say "What a nice day!" or "What a good idea!" Wonderful does not mean bad. I ask, I answer. Is wonderful good or bad? Good. Is "What a nice day!" happy or sad? Happy.`
2. `a beautiful day` — `Beautiful means very nice to see. A beautiful day has sun and blue sky. A beautiful flower is nice to see. A beautiful day is not a rainy day. I ask, I answer. Is a sunny day beautiful? Yes.`
3. `day and night` — `Day is when there is light. Night is when it is dark. The song talks about day and night. Remember the Bible: God called the light "day" and the dark "night." I ask, I answer. Is night light or dark? Dark.`
**Árnyékolás:** a **dal címe**, `What a wonderful world.`
**Feladat:** `Say two sentences about the song. Start: The song is ...`

## S2 (hétfő) — Let It Be — The Beatles

**Téma:** valaki nehéz időben megnyugtató tanácsot kap: ne küzdj mindennel, fogadd el. Lassú zongorás dal.
**Szókapcsolatok:**
1. `Let it be.` — `Let it be means: do not change it. Do not fight it. Accept the situation. If you are tired, you can let it be. If it rains, you let it be. It does not mean: break it. I ask, I answer. Does "let it be" mean fight or accept? Accept.`
2. `a hard time` — `A hard time is a time when life is difficult. Tom has a hard time when his account is locked. Maria has a hard time when she is sick. A hard time is not a holiday. I ask, I answer. Is a locked account a hard time at work? Yes, a small one.`
3. `Don't worry.` — `Don't worry means: do not be afraid or stressed. A friend says: Don't worry. It is OK. Don't worry is not the same as I do not care. I ask, I answer. Does "Don't worry" help a person or hurt a person? It helps.`
**Árnyékolás:** a cím, `Let it be.`
**Feladat:** `Say two sentences. The singer says ... / When I have a hard time, I ...`

## S3 (kedd) — Count on Me — Bruno Mars

**Téma:** két barát megígéri, hogy mindig számíthatnak egymásra. Vidám, egyszerű.
**Szókapcsolatok:**
1. `count on someone` — `Count on someone means: you know the person will help you. Tom can count on Anna. Anna helps him at the help desk. Count on is not the same as count numbers. I ask, I answer. If you can count on a friend, does the friend help you? Yes.`
2. `be there for someone` — `Be there for someone means: you are near and you help. A good friend is there for you. When Tom has a problem, Anna is there for him. I ask, I answer. Is a friend who is there for you a good friend? Yes.`
3. `a friend` — `A friend is a person you like and trust. You can talk to a friend. A friend is not a stranger. I ask, I answer. Is a stranger a friend? No.`
**Árnyékolás:** `Count on me.`
**Feladat:** `Say two sentences: The song is about ... / I can count on ...`

## S4 (szerda) — Lean on Me — Bill Withers

**Téma:** ha valaki gyenge pillanatban van, a másik felajánlja a segítségét. Lassú, ismétlődő.
**Szókapcsolatok:**
1. `lean on someone` — `Lean on someone means: you take help from a person when you are not strong. A tired man leans on a wall. Here, a friend is like a wall: you can lean on a friend. I ask, I answer. If you lean on a friend, do you get help? Yes.`
2. `strong` — `Strong means: not weak. A strong man can carry a heavy box. A strong person can also be strong in a hard time. Strong is the opposite of weak. I ask, I answer. Is strong the same as weak? No.`
3. `help someone` — `Help someone means: you do something for a person. Anna helps Tom with his password. Tom helps Maria with a box. Help is not hurt. I ask, I answer. Does Anna help Tom? Yes.`
**Árnyékolás:** `Lean on me.`
**Feladat:** `Say two sentences: The song is ... / I help ... at work.`
**Napló (szerda, 5'):** két–három mondat a héten tanult történetről.

## S5 (csütörtök) — Stand By Me — Ben E. King

**Téma:** hűség sötét időben is: maradj mellettem. Erős basszus, lassú tempó.
**Szókapcsolatok:**
1. `stand by someone` — `Stand by someone means: stay near a person and help in a hard time. A good friend stands by you. It does not mean: stand next to a door. I ask, I answer. Does a friend stand by you in a hard time? Yes.`
2. `afraid` — `Afraid means: you feel fear. A child is afraid of the dark. Do not be afraid is a nice sentence from a friend. Afraid is not the same as angry. I ask, I answer. Is afraid a feeling of fear or a feeling of joy? Fear.`
3. `stay with me` — `Stay with me means: do not go away. Stay here with me. Stay with me is not go away. I ask, I answer. Does "stay with me" mean go away or stay here? Stay here.`
**Árnyékolás:** `Stand by me.`
**Feladat:** `Say two sentences: The song is ... / A friend stands by me when ...`
**Kapcsolat a Bibliával:** `Teacher: In the Bible, Jesus says, "Do not be afraid. I am with you." This is a similar idea: stay with someone.` (Ez tanári mondat, nem WEB-szöveg.)
