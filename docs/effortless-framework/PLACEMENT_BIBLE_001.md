## Bible-track error patterns to train

1. Question comprehension: `Who / Where / What` heard and processed under time pressure.
2. Articles: `a` vs `the` (`Ahab was the bad king` → `a bad king`).
3. Simple past: `tried`, `saw`, `called`, `created`, `said`.
4. Core Bible words not secure: prayer, forgive, faith (and sound pair faith/face).
5. Sentences over about 8 words need chunked build-up.

## What worked in the same session

- Backward/forward chunk build-up (`Let there be light` → `God said, "Let there be light."`).
- One short task per turn once the task was stated clearly.
- Slow delivery with the learner controlling the next step.
- Genesis 1:1 spoken correctly after a repair on `the heavens`.

## Test design problems found (fix in v2)

- Items were not graded; item 4 jumped to a longer input.
- The test did not first teach the question words, so part of the result measures
  `Where/Who` familiarity, not Bible listening.
- Vocabulary part mixed meaning-in-Hungarian with meaning-in-English; score both.
- No separate note for recognition (understands the story) vs production.

- Most recognition correct, production weak: stay A1/A2, increase short output tasks.
- Questions fail, content known: train question words first (see Week 1 plan).
